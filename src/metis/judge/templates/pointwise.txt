Evaluate using these criteria, in priority order:
1. Correctness — factual accuracy; correct code/math. Any significant error caps the score at 4.
2. Instruction following — does it do what was actually asked?
3. Chatbot fit — follows the chatbot's own instructions (role, scope, format, language rules) and stays grounded in the retrieved context; invented facts count as errors.
4. Language — answers in the user's language; natural, fluent Persian when the user wrote Persian.
5. Helpfulness and completeness — addresses the real need.

7. Safety — penalize harmful content and unjustified refusals.

 3-4  major errors or misses the main point

 9-10 excellent: correct, complete, well-targeted, nothing meaningful to improve

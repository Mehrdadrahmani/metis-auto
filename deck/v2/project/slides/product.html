The working product, in Persian and English: a chat website for people and students, and an OpenAI-compatible API for companies. Every answer shows which model answered, its cost and the saving. What makes it different: the router is learned once, from real questions checked by a judge; when a new model comes out, it only answers our validation questions once to get its scores, with no retraining; every message goes to the best quality for the price across all providers; and if a provider fails, the next model answers, while repeated questions come from the cache.

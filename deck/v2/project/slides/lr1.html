The current approach

LLM Router

For the hard tenth of the traffic an LLM picks the model from retrieved evidence and says why. For the other 90% a rule decides, with no extra call at all — because the decision must never cost more than the answer.

This is the router Metis runs today, and the one the evaluation section compares against. Two things to hold on to before the numbers: the LLM only decides for about a tenth of the traffic, and every decision it makes carries a written reason. I will show the request path, where the evidence comes from, when the LLM is called, how health questions are handled, and then exactly where this approach wins and where it loses.

The answer to the iceberg: you ask your question as usual. TakRouter recognises what kind of question it is and picks the model with the best quality for the price on that kind of question, from the whole iceberg, not just the tip. You get the answer, which model answered, and how much you saved. When a better model appears, it joins in one step; if a provider goes down, the next model answers.

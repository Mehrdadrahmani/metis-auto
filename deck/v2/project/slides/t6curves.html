T5 · How we measure a router

Quality you get at every budget

AUDC = mean Q(b)

Q(b) = best quality at budget b, averaged over all budgets · higher is better

QNC = b* ÷ c(h†)

b* = smallest budget that matches the best model h† · lower is better (ours ≈ 4%)

[7] Validation, n = 798, held out by conversation (curves). Q̃(b) = best quality reachable at budget b; h† = best single model

A deferral curve shows the quality a router reaches at every budget, as we sweep lambda from cheap to expensive. Higher and further left is better. AUDC sums the whole curve into one number; QNC asks how much we must spend to match the best single model. The curves here are from validation; the sealed test gives the numbers on the next slide.

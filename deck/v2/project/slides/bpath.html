One message through the gateway, in 7 steps

Rules filter models that can serve the request. The embedding feeds both the cache and the router. Failover goes to the next-best model at another provider. Checks catch empty, cut-off or wrong-language answers: one repair, then a stronger model. Every request is traced with model, cost and time, never the text. Measured overhead: 23 ms at p50, 72 ms at p95.

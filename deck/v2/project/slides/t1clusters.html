Step one of UniRoute. Each question becomes a vector phi of x, its embedding. K-means groups 209,109 unlabeled train prompts into 8 topics, each with a centre mu k. The map shows our 798 validation prompts: similar questions sit together. A new message simply joins its nearest centre. We use only the user’s own text, not the chatbot’s instructions. The K-selection rule preferred 4; we shipped 8.

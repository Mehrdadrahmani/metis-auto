We earn a 20% fee on top of the model price, and the user sees the price and the saving on every answer. With the fee, a large customer pays about what always-Luna costs, but gets better quality, and 95% less than Sonnet. People top up credit and pay per message; developers pay the same way through the API; large companies can buy a self-hosted licence. The scenario is only an example: ten customers at a million messages a month would bring about 1,380 dollars a month.

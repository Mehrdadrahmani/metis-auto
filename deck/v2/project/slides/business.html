Our business model is simple: one router, three ways to pay. Everyday users and students use the Persian chat website: a few free messages every day, then prepaid credit in rial, with a student plan. Tech companies use the same router through an OpenAI-compatible API and pay per message: the model's price plus a small fee. Large or data-sensitive companies can run the router on their own servers with a yearly licence and support. Customers accept the fee because routing saves much more than it costs: on our test, routed messages cost 69 cents per thousand against 16 dollars for always using Claude Sonnet. We will set the exact fee and plan prices after our customer interviews.

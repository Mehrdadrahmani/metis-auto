How we make money. The customer pays the model price plus a 20 percent fee, in rial. That is inside the range local resellers charge today, 15 to 30 percent. Because most messages go to strong low-cost models, the customer still pays about 95 percent less than with always-Sonnet, even after our fee. With an assumed 1,800 dollars a year of model spend, one team brings about 360 dollars of gross profit a year. New customers get free starter credit; large companies can buy a self-hosted licence.

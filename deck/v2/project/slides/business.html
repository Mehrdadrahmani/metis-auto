Customers keep most of the saving; we keep a small fee on every message.

One router, three ways to pay. Everyday users and students use the Persian chat website: some free messages every day, then prepaid credit in rial; we reach them through universities, student groups and Persian social media. Tech companies use the API and pay the model price plus a small fee; we reach them through developer communities and pilot partners. Large or data-sensitive companies run the router on their own servers with a yearly licence. Customers accept the fee because routing saves much more than it costs. Exact fee and plan prices will be set after customer interviews.

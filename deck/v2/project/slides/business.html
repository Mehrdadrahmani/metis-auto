Business model

Free to start, pay as you grow

Value

Same quality, much lower cost; one place for every model; pay in rial.

Customers

Everyday users and students (chat); tech companies (API); large companies (own servers).

Revenue

Freemium chat → usage-based fee on every message; yearly licence for large companies.

Unit economics to measure in pilots: customer cost (CAC) and lifetime value (LTV), target LTV ≥ 3× CAC.

Three parts that lock together. Value: the same quality for much less money, every model in one place, and payment in rial. Customers: everyday users and students on the chat website, tech companies on the API, and large companies on their own servers. Revenue: the chat is free to start with daily messages; then usage-based: we keep a small fee on every message, which customers accept because routing saves much more than it costs; large companies pay a yearly licence. We do not know our customer acquisition cost and lifetime value yet; we will measure them in the pilots, with the usual target of lifetime value at least three times acquisition cost.

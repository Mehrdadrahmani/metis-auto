Metis routing · Golden Set, 100 samples · 3 Nov 2026

Conclusion: TakRouter is the better router for Metis. There is no router LLM call: routing is a fast lookup against learned clusters, so there is no extra model to pay for on every request. It picks models that succeed, by each model's track record per cluster: only 6 of 100 requests needed a second model, against 30. And it is better and cheaper together: higher quality in every product at half the cost, closing most of the gap to production answers. Recommendation: adopt TakRouter as Metis' default routing approach, and keep extending the Golden Set as traffic grows.

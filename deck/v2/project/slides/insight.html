On Persian exam questions with known answers, the winner changes by subject: DeepSeek for maths and physics, Luna for biology, Gemini for Persian literature. And price does not mean quality: on our test, Claude Sonnet cost 24 times DeepSeek and passed fewer answers. So the right model depends on the question, and paying for the famous one is often waste.

Public leaderboards, autumn 2026, not our data. For building websites and apps, Claude Opus 5.5 leads the Arena WebDev board clearly, 1810 against 1773 for GPT-6 Astra. For the hardest maths, OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol solves all of Epoch AI's FrontierMath tier 4 problems; Claude is close behind. For everyday chat, people vote Google's Gemini 4 Carbon first on Arena. For value, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash costs about 60 cents per task on the Artificial Analysis index, against almost 8 dollars for the top model, about 13 times cheaper. And two winners come from below the tip of the iceberg: Moonshot's Kimi leads agent and terminal tasks, and Alibaba's Qwen leads a test in 29 languages. Some of these leads are small and within noise; the point is that no single provider wins everywhere, so the right model depends on the question.

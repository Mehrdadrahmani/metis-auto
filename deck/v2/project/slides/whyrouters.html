Why routers at all? Because sending every message to the most expensive model wastes money. Berkeley's RouteLLM study cut cost by up to 85 percent while keeping 95 percent of GPT-4's quality. Amazon's own tests saved 48 percent at Claude Sonnet's quality. The big companies agree: OpenAI's GPT-5 routes between a fast and a thinking model, and Amazon and Microsoft sell routers in their clouds. And the market is real: OpenRouter, a model marketplace, has 8 million users and 100 trillion tokens a month. But each big player only routes between its own models. TakRouter routes across all providers, for Persian products, and Iranian teams can actually use and pay for it.

Validation, n = 798, held out (2-fold split by conversation). Test-set curves are not drawn: the test set stays sealed

The deferral curve is the main measure in the UniRoute paper. Each router is run at many values of lambda; each point is the average cost and pass rate at that setting. A curve that rises fast on the left gives good quality cheaply. All learned routers climb to DeepSeek’s level at about 4% of Sonnet’s cost, and K = 4 keeps rising towards Sonnet’s quality. The differences between routers are small on this data. Only validation curves are shown; the sealed test gives the summary numbers on the next slide.

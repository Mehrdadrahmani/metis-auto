Step three, the online rule from the UniRoute paper. A message is embedded, assigned to its nearest topic k, and for every model h we add its error rate in that topic to lambda times its cost. The smallest score wins. In the health example, DeepSeek and Sonnet have the same error, 21.5%, but Sonnet’s cost term is 0.515 against 0.021, so DeepSeek wins clearly. Lambda is the dial between quality and cost; the website modes change it. It was chosen out of fold on validation so the router does not fall below always-DeepSeek.

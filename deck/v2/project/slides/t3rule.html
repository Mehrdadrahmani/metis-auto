Step three, the rule from the UniRoute paper. A message is embedded and assigned to its nearest topic k; for every model h we add its error in that topic to lambda times its normalised cost, and the smallest score wins. In health and medicine, DeepSeek and Sonnet have the same error, 21.5%, but Sonnet’s cost term is 0.515 against 0.021, so DeepSeek wins clearly. Lambda is the dial between quality and cost; the website’s Economy, Balanced and Best quality modes move it. It was chosen out of fold on validation so the router does not fall below always-DeepSeek.

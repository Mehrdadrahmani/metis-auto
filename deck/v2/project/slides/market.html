Market size. Top down, the world spends about 28 billion dollars on generative AI models this year. Gateways and routers are a market of their own, growing about 35% a year. Bottom up, which is what counts for us: Iran has about 19,000 app teams and 10,000 knowledge-based companies; we assume about 25,000 teams overall, one in five building AI features, each spending about 1,800 dollars a year on models. That is about 9 million dollars a year of model spend in Iran today, growing fast. Our three-year goal is 500 teams, about 0.9 million dollars of spend through TakRouter. These are assumptions, written down so they can be challenged.

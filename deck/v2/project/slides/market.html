The market from the top and from the bottom. From the top: Menlo Ventures counts 177 billion dollars of enterprise AI spending this year, and 86 percent of the money for model APIs goes to just two providers, the tip of the iceberg. From the bottom, the part we can actually serve: Iran has about 19,000 app teams and 10,000 knowledge-based companies; we assume about 25,000 teams overall, one in five building AI features, each spending about 1,800 dollars a year on models. That is about 9 million dollars a year from tech companies. Then people and students: about 5 percent of Iranian adults, some 3.6 million, use AI tools often; if 2 percent of them pay about 3 dollars a month, that is another 2.6 million. Together about 12 million dollars a year in Iran today, and growing. Our three-year goal is 500 tech teams and 20,000 paying users and students, about 1.6 million dollars a year through TakRouter. The Iran numbers are assumptions; our customer interviews will test them.

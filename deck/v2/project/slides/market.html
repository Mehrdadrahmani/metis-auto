Bottom-up, starting with the money we can really get. Our income is our 20 percent fee, 14 cents per thousand messages, measured on our test. Tech companies: about 5,000 Iranian teams build AI features; at half a million messages a month each, one team brings 828 dollars a year; at 10 percent adoption that is 0.41 million dollars a year. Users and students: about 3.6 million Iranians use AI often; at 20 messages a day, one person brings about 1 dollar a year in fees; at 10 percent that is 0.36 million. Together our target, the SOM, is 0.77 million dollars a year. The serviceable market in Iran is 7.7 million a year in fees, and the world spends 177 billion dollars on enterprise AI. Honest note: per user, the fee is small, so the user plan will need a subscription; we will test that in customer interviews. Message volumes are assumptions.

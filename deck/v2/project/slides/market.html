The market is huge and growing: Menlo Ventures estimates 177 billion dollars of enterprise AI spending in 2026, and 86% of the money for model APIs goes to just two providers, the tip of the iceberg. We earn a 20% fee on top of the model price, shown on every answer. Even with the fee, a customer sending a million messages a month pays about 828 dollars instead of 16,320 with Sonnet. We start in Iran, where global routers are hard to pay for, then the region; large companies can self-host.

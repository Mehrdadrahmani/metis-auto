From one message to the market. We earn 14 cents for every thousand messages. Iran has two groups for us. Tech companies: about 5,000 teams building AI features; if each sends half a million messages a month, the whole group is worth about 4.1 million dollars a year to us. Everyday users and students: about 3.6 million Iranians use AI often; at 20 messages a day, about 3.6 million dollars a year. Together about 7.7 million. Step one: win 10 percent of tech companies, 0.41 million dollars a year. Step two: add 10 percent of users and students, 0.77 million a year. Then the Persian-speaking region, inside a world AI market of 177 billion dollars. Message volumes are our assumptions; the price per message is measured. Sources: income = 20% fee = $0.14 per 1,000 messages [4]; ~5,000 AI teams × 500k messages a month [17]; 3.6M frequent AI users × 20 messages a day [16]; volumes assumed; [6] Menlo Ventures, Nov 2026.

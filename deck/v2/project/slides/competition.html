Global gateways like OpenRouter are big and growing, but you choose the model and they do not serve Iran. Learned routers like Martian and Not Diamond are closest to us but are not available here and not tuned to Persian. Cloud routers only pick inside one company's models. Our edge is the combination: learned per-message routing across providers, a Persian evaluation set, rial payment and failover. Our toughest honest competitor today is simply always using DeepSeek; we win when traffic is varied.

JPMorgan warns against claiming a blue ocean, so we show the real alternatives. Most people stay with one famous model: Luna or Sonnet. Sonnet costs 24 times more for no better quality on our data. Our toughest competitor is always-DeepSeek, which ties us today. But DeepSeek is today’s best cheap model, not necessarily tomorrow’s: TakRouter brings in the next better model with one fingerprint, and a single model is a single point of failure. Global routers like OpenRouter have many models, but the user has to choose, they are not measured on Persian, and paying them from Iran is hard.

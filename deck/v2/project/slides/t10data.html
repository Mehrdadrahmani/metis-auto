T10 · Limits of our data

Better data, better routing: Khayyam shows it

Why our chatbot data was easy

• Chatbots with fixed roles (support, companion)

• General, easy questions; no hard ones

• Almost no code

→ only two models compete; after cost, DeepSeek wins

On Khayyam, hard subject questions with true answers, the winner changes by subject, and routing gains +0.020 area (CI +0.001 to +0.044). With harder, more varied data, TakRouter pays off more.

Left: validation, n = 798, AI judge · Right: Khayyam test, n = 1,100, true answers, four models without Sonnet

Why was the gain small on our main data? The chatbots had fixed roles, like customer support or a companion, and users asked general, easy questions with almost no code. So in every topic only DeepSeek and Sonnet compete, and once cost counts, DeepSeek wins almost everywhere: routing becomes almost the same as always-DeepSeek. Khayyam is different: hard exam questions in clear subjects, with true answers. There the best model changes by subject (DeepSeek for maths and physics, Luna for biology, Gemini for Persian literature) and routing showed a real gain. Pilot partners with harder, more varied traffic are how we get more value.

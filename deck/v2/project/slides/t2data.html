T2 · Data and evaluation

Real traffic, a sealed test opened once

Golden rules: no choice made with test data · every model and judge call cached · seeds recorded · prompts over 20,000 characters excluded.

Our data: 338 thousand real prompts from Persian chatbots. The large unlabeled part only teaches the topics. 800 validation prompts were answered by all four models and judged; every choice, the number of topics, lambda, thresholds, was made there. The 300-prompt test set stayed sealed until the router was frozen and was opened once.

Smart LLM gateway for Persian AI products

TakRouter

Every message goes to the best-value model, from every provider.

Team Synapse · Rahnema College 2026 · Presenter: Mehrdad Rahmani · November 2026

Open with the one-line promise. TakRouter is a gateway that Persian chatbots and apps call instead of one AI model. For every message it picks the model that gives a good answer for the lowest price, from many providers, and it keeps working when one provider fails. Then go straight to the problem.

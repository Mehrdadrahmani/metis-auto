Lambda is the price of quality. Every model gets a score, its error in the question's topic plus lambda times its price, and the lowest score wins. Take a humanities and religion question. With lambda zero only quality counts, and Sonnet wins with the lowest error, 0.42. With the shipped lambda, 0.515, Sonnet's price adds 0.515 to its score, so its three points of quality are not worth twenty times the price, and Luna wins with 0.48. With lambda three, price dominates and the cheapest model, Gemini, wins. These are the website's three modes: best quality, balanced and economy. We chose 0.515 on validation questions the router had not seen: among all lambdas that stay at least as good as always using DeepSeek, we took the cheapest.

Who has the pain: Iranian teams that run chatbots and AI features, for support, education, shops and bots on Bale or Telegram. Three problems. First, access: the big providers do not serve Iran and cards are sanctioned, so teams buy through local resellers that add 15 to 30 percent. Second, habit: they send every message to one famous model, even a greeting. They cannot test hundreds of models themselves. Third, reliability: one provider link is a single point of failure, and this year showed how fragile the connection is. The cost of the habit, measured on 299 real chatbot messages: Claude Sonnet cost 24 times DeepSeek and passed fewer answers.
